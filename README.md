# Market Event Signals

**Market Event Signals is a Telegram app that turns market-moving events into stock and ETF exposure maps.**

It helps traders, investors, and market watchers understand how news events may affect U.S. stocks, ETFs, sectors, and broader market themes.

Instead of only showing that something happened, Market Event Signals explains what the event may touch in the market.

## How it works

The idea is simple:

**event → market mechanism → exposed assets**

A headline tells you what happened.

Market Event Signals helps show what that headline can affect.

---

## What we track

Examples of market-moving event chains:

* **Oil / Strait of Hormuz risk** → energy, airlines, shipping, inflation-sensitive assets, defense
* **Jobs data** → Fed expectations, yields, dollar, gold, banks, tech
* **AI chip news** → semiconductors, data centers, power demand, cloud capex
* **Tariffs and trade policy** → autos, retail, industrials, supply chains
* **Currency moves** → dollar, yen, exporters, ETFs, global risk appetite
* **Geopolitical events** → commodities, volatility, currencies, supply routes, sector exposure

---

## Signal format

A typical signal maps:

* **Event**
* **Market mechanism**
* **Potentially exposed stocks and ETFs**
* **Context / reasoning**

---

## Example

**Event:** Oil supply risk cools around the Strait of Hormuz

**Market mechanism:** Lower supply-disruption fear can affect oil prices, inflation expectations, yields, and sector rotation.

**Potential exposures:** Energy, airlines, transport, defense, gold, bonds, volatility.

---

## Why this exists

Market information is technically public.

But public information is not always usable information.

The useful map is often scattered across headlines, analyst notes, macro threads, ETF baskets, sector models, and people who already know what to look for.

Market Event Signals is built to make that event-to-asset map easier to read.

---

## Use cases

Market Event Signals can help users:

* understand which assets may be exposed to market-moving events;
* follow event-driven trading ideas;
* monitor stocks and ETFs connected to major news;
* save time on manual market research;
* get market context directly in Telegram;
* build watchlists around current events and sector exposure.
